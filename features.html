Unified POS

Fast checkout for every branch

A fast point-of-sale for each branch that works with your inventory, loyalty and online orders.

  • Barcode based billing at the counter
  • Stock updates the moment a sale is made
  • Works with loyalty and online orders
02
Multi-Branch Inventory

Real-time stock across every branch

See stock across every branch and warehouse in one place, with transfer requests and low-stock alerts.

  • Live stock for every branch and warehouse
  • Branch to branch transfer requests
  • Low-stock alerts before items run out
03
Customer Profiles

Know your customers, serve them better

Keep customer details and purchase history in one profile so every branch and your online store recognise the same shopper.

  • One profile shared across all channels
  • Purchase history available at the counter
  • Supports your loyalty programme

AI Visual Search

Find it by photo, instantly

Customers upload a photo and instantly find matching or similar items from your live stock.

  • Search by outfit photo instead of keywords
  • Matches against live stock only
  • Highlights what customers ask for but you do not stock

3D Smart Fitting

Try it on before they buy

Shoppers preview garments on a 3D avatar, which helps them pick the right size and cuts returns.

  • 3D avatar preview on mobile
  • Better size choices for shoppers
  • Fewer returns for your store

Analytics & Insights
