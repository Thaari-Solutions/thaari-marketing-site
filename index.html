The Retail Challenge

Why Growing Clothing Brands Need a Unified System

Managing multiple store locations with separate billing counters and disconnected online catalogs creates daily friction for both staff and shoppers.

Inventory Gap

Disconnected Branch Stock

When billing and inventory records are isolated by location, cashiers cannot check availability at sister branches and online shoppers risk ordering out-of-stock sizes.

Discovery Gap

Untapped Social Media Demand

Customers frequently shop with outfit screenshots from Instagram, TikTok, or Facebook, but standard text search bars cannot match photos against store inventory.

Conversion Gap

Online Fit & Styling Uncertainty

Shoppers abandon carts when they are unsure how a size will fit their body measurements or how individual tops, trousers, and footwear look together as an outfit.

Analytics Gap

Invisible Buying Trends

When a customer searches for a trending style or color that your store does not carry yet, traditional platforms lose that visitor without recording what they wanted to buy.

Virtual Fitting Room

3D Mannequin Sizing & Complete Outfit Styling

Give online shoppers the confidence to buy with an interactive 3D mannequin that adapts to their body measurements and suggests matching pieces from your catalog.

  • Personalized Size Calculator: Compares customer height, chest, and waist measurements against your garment size charts for accurate fit advice.
  • Two-Way Outfit Recommendations: Automatically pairs shirts with matching trousers and shoes—or matches tops starting from a selected pair of pants.
  • One-Click Outfit Checkout: Shoppers can add the entire coordinated look to their cart or reserve it for an in-store fitting session.

Omnichannel Retail

Unified Multi-Branch POS & Click-and-Try

Keep every physical billing counter and your online store connected in real time, eliminating double-selling while offering flexible fulfilment options.
